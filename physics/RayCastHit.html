RayCastHit

Class

The result of a successful cast, describing exactly where the ray hit, what it hits and how.
If Cast() returns false, treat hitInfo as null, none of its fields are meaningful.

Point and Distance tell you where the hit happened, in world space and as a distance along the ray. Collider gives you the shape that was hit, and through Collider.gameObject the GameObject it belongs to. Everything you'd normally look up with GetComponent() is reachable from there.

Normal is the direction the surface faces at the exact point of impact, a vector of length 1, pointing away from the shape, perpendicular to its surface there.

Using Normal to place and orient an object 
//Drop a decal exactly on the ground below this point
if (RayCast.Cast(spawnPoint, Vector3.Down, 10f, out RaycastHit hit))
    decal.transform.SetPosition(hit.Point);

//Telling a walkable floor apart from a steep wall using the normal alone
if (RayCast.Cast(origin, Vector3.Down, 0.3f, out RaycastHit groundHit))
    bool walkable = groundHit.Normal.Y > 0.7f;

Properties

Distance float Distance from the ray's origin to the hit point
Collider Collider3D The Collider that was hit
Origin Vector3 The origin of the ray that hit the collider
Direction Vector3 The direction of the ray that hit the collider
